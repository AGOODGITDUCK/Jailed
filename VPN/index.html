How to Turn On/Off VPN

Once you have installed the Jailed Profile with the VPN configuration:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap VPN & Device Management → VPN.
  3. Select the VPN you want to connect (e.g., Tokyo VPN).
  4. Toggle the switch to connect or disconnect the VPN.

⚠️ VPN cannot be toggled directly from the web page for security reasons. You must do it from Settings.
